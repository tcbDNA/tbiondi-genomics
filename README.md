# tbiondi-genomics

Computational genome projects by Thomas Biondi, done by Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5 agents and checked by AI,
not reviewed by a human; no lab work.

## Projects

- [grigorovia-mito-insertions](grigorovia-mito-insertions/): A census of 20,343 mitochondrial genomes for
  codon-skipping lineages; nothing new under frozen rules, plus one exploratory yeast candidate (*Grigorovia
  jiainica*).
- [metazoan-mito-uaa-tyr](metazoan-mito-uaa-tyr/): A test, under rules fixed in advance, of whether tRNA-Tyr U33C goes
  with UAA reassigned from stop to tyrosine in 32,982 public mitogenomes, and mtRF1a T191S only in flagged species and
  sisters. Zero new reassigned clades under the locked rules; 17 of 28,583 readable UAA=Stop genomes have a non-U33
  tRNA-Tyr call, of which 13 have Tier 1–3 support, 3 are unconfirmed and 1 is dropped.
