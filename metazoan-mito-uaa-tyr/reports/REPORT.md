## Abstract
We asked whether two changes proposed to drive mitochondrial UAA reassignment to tyrosine track the code in public data: tRNA-Tyr(GUA) U33C (Lin et al. 2026; Zhao et al. 2026) and mtRF1a T191S, human T216 (Zhao et al. 2026). The scope is 32,982 public mitogenomes under rules fixed in `PREREG.md`, and mtRF1a only in flagged species and sisters.

**[C]** Zero new reassigned clades under the locked rules. One non-panel genome, *Rotylenchulus reniformis*, met the Tyr rule; it fails only the locked model-column count of anticodon-stem pairs in its tRNA-Tyr (3 of 5), so it is an "unconfirmed single-genome candidate" in PREREG §3's terms. **[P]** A check whose criteria were written before mapping finds a real mitochondrial U33 tRNA-Tyr locus there: 545 of 545 reads read U33, the assembled flanks are identical to the record, and both callers' boundaries meet the structural criterion. The two boundaries differ, so the boundary is unresolved, and about 17% of random stretches of this mitogenome would meet the acceptor-stem part of that criterion. The genome stays unconfirmed by the locked rule. NCBI Taxonomy already assigns the genus *Rotylenchulus* code 14 (UAA = Tyr), and the flatworm-code reading of its COI was reported for *R. borealis* (Vovlas et al. 2026).

**[C]** 17 of 28,583 readable UAA=Stop genomes (0.059% per genome, 95% CI 0.037–0.095%; 0.062% per taxon) have a non-U33 tRNA-Tyr call: 14 with C33 and 3 with G33. "Stop" rests on the internal-UAA counter: Codetta returned "?" for 28,580 of the 28,583. **[P]** Under one evidence rule, 13 of the 17 have Tier 1–3 support, 3 are unconfirmed and 1 is dropped. The C33 genomes are 6 with same-species independent support (4 lineages), 3 with reads from their own specimen only, 2 lineage-backed only, 2 unconfirmed and 1 dropped. The G33 genomes are 1 with independent support and 1 with own-specimen reads (both *Craniella*, 1 genus), and 1 unconfirmed.

**[P]** C33 is not sufficient for UAA=Tyr under the vertebrate mitochondrial code (code 2). This rests on the 6 independently supported C33 genomes, all vertebrates. It needs C33 to be the dominant tRNA-Tyr allele with no second functional copy, not zero U33 reads; the read and contig searches found no second copy in any of the six (§7.2.10). The contig search detects copies at roughly half the mitochondrial depth or more. It used pre-repeat-resolution contigs for *M. kubotai* and *A. glossodonta*, a whole-genome-amplified library from a different fish for *A. glossodonta*, and, for *M. kubotai*, a library from a fish whose mitogenome differs from the counted record by 2.4%. G33 is tolerated with UAA=Stop in one sponge genus, *Craniella* (2 species, code 4), with two caveats for *C. infrequens*: its independent RNA reads match a nuclear copy as well as they match the mitogenome, and the upper bound on a U33 share in its reads is above 4%. **[C]** Among 16,902 resolved UAA=Stop genomes with code 5, including 1,491 molluscs (489 bivalves), 2 have a C33 call, neither in a mollusc; **[P]** one is unconfirmed (*Toxocara*) and one was dropped (*Globodera*), so none is confirmed. Lin et al.'s statement is limited to species "retaining the canonical code 5", and no confirmed genome here contradicts it. **[C]** None of 77 resolved starfish has C33; **[P]** the figures Li et al. 2019 cite for a starfish C show only hemichordates, and the one starfish in their accession table is U33 here.

**Calibration [panel, descriptive; prior art]:** U33 occurs in UAA=Tyr lineages, and T216 occurs in UAA=Tyr lineages, so neither change is necessary. Serine at 216 occurs in two calibration lineages (Anomiidae with Placunidae, and *Cephalodiscus*) and, **[E]**, in the unconfirmed candidate *Rotylenchulus*; none of the 47 UAA=Stop species with both a UAA call and a readable mtRF1a position 216 has S216 (34 Gate 2 background species, *Heteranomia squamula*, *Pododesmus macrochisma* and 11 in Gate 4; two more background species show only the paralogue MTRF1 and are not counted; §6). The panel pass rate for the molecular gates (UAA rule plus tRNA-Tyr gate and audit) is 14 of 20 known positives, against 20 of 20 for the UAA rule alone, and it is zero for *Enigmonia* and *Radopholus*. A null on new clades is therefore weak, and the panel does not test the novelty and replication parts of PREREG §3.

## 1. Introduction
Zhao et al. 2026 (BMC Biology, `10.1186/s12915-026-02767-5`) propose that tRNA-Tyr(GUA) U33C and mtRF1a T191S drive UAA reassignment in several lineages. Lin et al. 2026 (bioRxiv `10.64898/2026.08.14.744788`) report a tRNA-Tyr change they call "U36C"; we infer (GATE_0, not stated by Lin) that this is canonical position 33. Lin write: "This substitution is present exclusively in the TAA=Tyr lineages and is absent in all species retaining the canonical code 5". Li et al. 2019 report a C in starfish tRNA-Tyr (§5f). This study checks the tRNA claim in public mitogenomes that met the eligibility filters (§2), and the release factor in flagged species and their sisters.

Prior art on *Rotylenchulus*. NCBI Taxonomy (the copy on disk) assigns mitochondrial code 14, in which UAA is tyrosine, to the genus *Rotylenchulus* and to *Radopholus similis*, *R. duriophilus*, *R. arabocoffeae*, *Helicotylenchus oleae*, *Ditylenchus dipsaci* and *D. gigas*, among others. `transl_table` 14 on GenBank records follows this assignment, so it is not independent evidence of a reassignment (*Ditylenchus* and *Radopholus* fragments without UAA carry it). Jacob et al. 2009 (read by keyword search in Gate 0 and here) established UAA=Tyr in *Radopholus similis* and *R. arabocoffeae*. Vovlas et al. 2026 (Plants 15:1423, `10.3390/plants15101423`; full text read) state that translating COI of *R. borealis* showed the use of the flatworm mitochondrial code (table 14), infer that two internal TAGs in a short haplotype "probably" code for tyrosine, "as does TAA stop codon in Radopholus similis", and raise heteroplasmy or a pseudogene as alternatives. They cite Palomares-Rius et al. 2021 and Torres et al. 2022, which I could not read. COI-barcode work therefore recognised the unusual code in *Rotylenchulus* before this study; what this study adds is mitogenome-wide, read-confirmed internal UAA and the release-factor genotype.

## 2. Methods and detection limits
Rules are in `PREREG.md` (lock `5aa5774`); code and outputs are in the commits in §10. Units: genomes (or species) and lineages are counted separately; origins are minimum changes on NCBI taxonomy, which is not a molecular phylogeny, so origin counts are approximate.
- **Units.** Counts below are genomes (one GenBank or RefSeq record), not species: the 28,583 resolved Stop genomes are 28,242 distinct sequences from 27,618 NCBI taxa. Among the 32,623 confirmatory genomes (31,564 taxa), 539 taxa have more than one genome (1,059 extra); **[P]** all 539 have open-nomenclature or hybrid names, which PREREG §1 keeps separate. 402 records are identical in sequence to another (297 pairs are a RefSeq and an INSDC record with identical sequence that the de-duplication did not merge), and 303 RefSeq records are derived from another included accession. PREREG words T1 for species; the test was run on records, so the interval and the denominator are per record. The non-U33 rate is 0.059% per genome, 0.060% per distinct sequence, 0.062% per taxon. UAA=Stop species are counted as species only for mtRF1a, where each species was read once.
- **Mitogenomes.** 32,982 included; 98 excluded with reasons; 32,623 confirmatory (32,624 with the named extra `AP002973`). 8,189 UNVERIFIED-flagged and 5,635 incomplete INSDC-only entries were not eligible. These are entries, not species: the 12,884 eligible INSDC-only entries are 12,089 taxa, and the 8,189 UNVERIFIED ones 7,345. The eligibility step was a one-off command; it is now scripted and reproduces every row (`src/cr2_repro.py elig`) **[P]**.
- **What the title filter lets in [P].** "Complete" is a title rule, not a check of content. Of the 28,583 resolved Stop genomes, 26 have titles that list genes (23 of them carry 12 or 13 annotated protein genes; `OQ946609`, `OQ946610` and `DQ007340` are real fragments), 8 say "partial" or "nearly complete" (RefSeq titles), 7 are one chromosome of a multipartite or numbered mitogenome (5 with a partial gene set), and 9 make no claim but carry 13 protein genes. Of the 17 screen positives one is affected, *Globodera* (chromosome I). `results/posthoc2/title_audit.tsv`.
- **UAA call.** Tyr needs ≥5 internal TAA in ≥3 genes, ≥3 at conserved-Tyr columns, LLR ≥3 and Codetta Y. **Stop calls rest on the internal-UAA counter.** The rule also asks that Codetta not say Y, but Codetta returned "?" for 28,580 of the 28,583 resolved Stop genomes (E for 2, Q for 1), so it adds no support for Stop **[P]**. "Stop" is an operational class: few or no internal UAA in the aligned genes. Both callers use the same OXPHOS profiles, the counter reads every taxon with table 5 and can keep more than one domain per gene, and the false-Stop rate was measured only by the planted series (§2.1).
- **tRNA-Tyr.** U33 at score ≥20 needs no second caller; any non-U33 needs a second caller and ≥4 of 5 stem pairs. Detection is therefore asymmetric, and recall for non-U33 was never measured (cold review #27). The callers are not treated alike: ARAGORN must place G34 within 2 nt on the same strand, tRNAscan-SE needs only interval overlap; with any annotation present, tRNA calls are not used in the overlap test; and the top covariance-model arm is chosen before the gate. What these choices change is measured in §7.2.9.
- **mtRF1a.** Positions 203 and 216 of human `NP_061914.3`; annotated proteins, then transcript sets, then assemblies, then reads (PREREG §2.4).

**PREREG §2.3, the audit that gates the new-clade endpoint**, in full: "Locus-identity audit (applied to every call, U33 and non-U33 alike; **required before any genome counts as a counterexample or new clade**): verified GUA in a 7-nt loop; ≥4 of 5 anticodon-stem pairs, with G·U wobble counted as paired; overlap ≤10 nt with any annotated CDS or other tRNA (…); and, if the record annotates a trnY, >50% overlap with it on the same strand."

- **B1 (confirmatory, computed after the review; Gate 3's code applied the ≥3-gene minimum to the Tyr species too, so it reported "not testable"):** testable as PREREG is worded, where the minimum is on the sister taxon. *R. reniformis* has 2 terminal calls (COX3, CYTB), both TAA: TAG share **0 of 2**, against a mean of **0.314** in 291 order-level Stop sisters (282 taxa; `src/cr2_repro.py b1`). It is a terminal-codon proxy on two genes: "first" labels the first in-frame TAA or TAG after the last aligned codon and does not establish termination. This is **uninformative**: with two calls, 0 of 2 would happen by chance 47% of the time at a share of 0.314 (0.686²). Sign test over 1 origin, p = 1.0. The first-stop rule captures in-frame UAAs just past the aligned gene ends, which under UAA=Tyr are not stops; TAG-first finds none (post-hoc transcript finding in §7.2.5).
- **Number of tests with a p-value: 2** (A1 and B1, both p = 1.0, both on the one blocked genome). Fisher, T2 and B3/B4 were not inferential. No correction was applied across the named tests (PREREG §6).

### a. C33 is not sufficient for UAA=Tyr under vertebrate code 2

## 8. Limitations
1. Detection: §2.1. The panel pass rate for the molecular gates is 14 of 20; the UAA thresholds were set with positives in view.
2. Excluded genomes: 8,189 UNVERIFIED-flagged entries (in-frame stops can cause the flag; *Anomia* and *Placuna* carry it) and 5,635 incomplete; "complete" is a title filter (§2); the exploratory run answers this in part.
3. Non-U33 calls need a second caller and U33 ≥20 does not, so non-U33 is easier to miss; its recall was not measured.
4. Candidates are single species; taxonomy stands in for phylogeny (the eel tree is weak); the T1 interval treats related species as independent.
5. Post-hoc audits followed seeing the calls, including the tier rule.
6. NUMTs were not checked in *Microdevario*, the Serrivomeridae, the singletons or *A. vulpes*.
7. Panel genomes support c and part of d; those are not new findings for those lineages.
8. mtRF1a is untested in *Pseudotanais*, *Placuna placenta*, *Microdevario*, Serrivomeridae, and (skipped) *Globodera*.
9. The *Rotylenchulus* tRNA-Tyr has two boundary maps that cannot be told apart (§7.2.11); its fragments may include pseudogenes or heteroplasmy (Vovlas et al.). The genome and read evidence for UAA is independent of the tRNA.
10. Stop rests on the internal-UAA counter (§2). Weak, context-specific or rare sense decoding is outside its resolution, and the false-Stop rate is not measured beyond the planted series.
11. The earlier read checks bound a minor U33 allele at 0.26% to 9% where there are more than 10 reads (§7.2.1), and the second-copy searches have the limits in §7.2.10.

## 9. Discussion
C33 is not sufficient for UAA=Tyr under the vertebrate code: 6 independently supported genomes in 4 vertebrate lineages have C33 with UAA=Stop, and 3 more rest on their own specimen. The claim needs C33 to be the dominant allele with no second functional copy (§5a). G33 is also tolerated with UAA=Stop (2 *Craniella* genomes, 1 genus), with the two *C. infrequens* caveats of §5b. None of this reaches code 5, the domain of Lin et al.'s statement, where no confirmed C33 Stop genome was found among 16,902. Neither change is necessary in the calibration set, and *Radopholus*, a UAA=Tyr lineage, has neither, so the two-part model (both required) is contradicted there, as prior work already showed. S216 is found in two calibration lineages and one unconfirmed candidate; none of the 47 UAA=Stop species checked has S216, so sufficiency is untested. *Rotylenchulus* has strong read-based internal UAA, S216, and a mitochondrial U33 tRNA-Tyr locus whose boundary is unresolved; it fails only the locked stem count and is an unconfirmed single-genome candidate until a dated amendment is tested on new data.
