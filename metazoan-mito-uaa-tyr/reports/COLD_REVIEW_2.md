# Independent cold audit of d1_v2.zip

## 1. Executive Verdict

The archive supports a large, reproducible **record-level screen** and a narrow biological conclusion: C33 in mitochondrial tRNA-Tyr can coexist with canonical UAA termination in vertebrates. The strongest fish dossiers carry independent evidence for C33, no detected internal UAA, 13 profile-detected coding genes and multiple predicted terminal UAAs. The main sweep also yields one strong UAA-as-Tyr candidate, *Rotylenchulus reniformis*, with DNA and RNA support for internal UAA. Its tRNA fails the locked model-column stem gate, so the operational result is zero new clades and one candidate. The headline 17/28,583 is an automated candidate-call rate. It includes a known duplicate-locus case and several unconfirmed calls, and its interval does not handle related genomes or uneven detection. The study does not establish a code-5 counterexample, a reliable metazoan prevalence, or a test of mtRF1a sufficiency. I could reproduce the main arithmetic from the archive, but missing raw and post-review files prevent independent re-execution of several decisive checks.

## 2. Critical Issues

**1. The new-clade result turns on a tRNA model failure, and 14/20 measures only part of the claimed endpoint.**  
`PREREG.md` §§2.3, 3 and `REPORT.md` §§2.1, 3.6, 4.1, 7.2.2. The `CM003310.1` master row has 32 internal TAA across six genes, 15 at Tyr columns, LLR 67.43 and Codetta Y. The WGS log gives 10,613 TAA versus 23 TAT/TAC counts across all 32 sites, and the RNA log gives 55 TAA counts across 16 sites. `results/gate3/stem_direct.tsv` records 3/5 at model columns versus 5/5 for the contiguous arm. The dossier gives a 53-nt model call and a 62-nt ARAGORN call with different boundaries. Keeping the locked call is procedurally correct. The genome is a positive sequence-based candidate, and the two boundary maps leave its tRNA unresolved. Separately, I reproduced **14/20** from the intersection of a Gate 2 tRNA call and an audit pass. All 20 are known, excluded controls. That calculation does not exercise the novelty, cross-species or independent-replication branches of §3. The Abstract and Figure 3 should call it *panel pass rate for the molecular gates*. The claimed new-clade null is particularly weak because `Enigmonia` and `Radopholus` have zero panel passes, and the locked method rejects many taxa or cannot evaluate them at all. The joint §3 null is already rejected on the counterexample side, as the revised report correctly says.

**2. The locked prevalence numerator contains a demonstrably ambiguous genome-level counterexample.**  
`REPORT.md` Abstract, §§4.2, 5a, 7.2.1, 7.2.7 and the `KU726971.1` dossier, lines 19–26 and 45–50. *Globodera ellingtonae* enters T1 as C33 from chromosome I, at score 10.0 with one corroborating caller. Chromosome II (`KU726972.1`, 6699–6752) has a U33 Tyr locus placed at the same coordinates by Infernal and ARAGORN. The 176 and 271 C33 read counts validate the local sequence, while neither establishes that it is the sole functional Tyr tRNA. The drop is well motivated. The result should consistently distinguish **17 locked screen positives, 13 with Tier 1–3 support, three unconfirmed, one dropped**. Within code 5 there are two screen positives, one dropped and one unconfirmed. The assertion that all 14 C33 hits meet a biological sufficiency-counterexample definition in §5a goes beyond the evidence. The vertebrate conclusion survives on the independently supported fish.

**3. The confirmatory sampling unit changed, and the staged inventory does not implement a single species-level selection.**  
`PREREG.md` §§1, 4, 6, `src/gate1_overlap.py` lines 19–38, `src/gate1b_sweep_set.py` lines 7–29, `src/gate3_classify.py` lines 60, 72–86, and `REPORT.md` §2. **6. The locus gate applies different corroboration and boundary tests to different records.**  
`PREREG.md` §2.3, `src/trny_decide.py` lines 7–12, `src/gate3_classify.py` lines 21–46, `src/gate3_features_fix.py` lines 6–14, and `src/trny_extract.py` lines 45–57. ARAGORN requires a same-strand G34 within two bases, while tRNAscan-SE needs only interval overlap. I ran a synthetic check in which an exact antisense tRNAscan interval returned corroboration. With no annotation, the overlap fallback checks only same-strand ARAGORN non-GUA hits, omits tRNAscan non-GUA hits and is skipped whenever any CDS or tRNA annotation is present, even if incomplete. Split features are scored one segment at a time, and same-locus overlap is measured against the shorter interval or segment. The extractor chooses its top plausible CM arm before the locus/corroboration checks, with no retry on the runner-up if that top arm fails. These behaviors can miss real trnY or accept a wrong locus. Their actual effect on the sweep cannot be quantified from the archive. Coverage varies sharply by phylum, and the panel's 26/273 misses rise to 33/273 with wrong-locus calls included, even before counting all final locus-audit failures.

**7. The UAA calibration does not establish out-of-sample accuracy for the combined pipeline or measure the false-Stop rate.**  
`src/uaa_caller.py` lines 9–16, 40–73, 83–89, `src/gate3_classify.py` lines 9–20, `src/lolo.py` lines 12–35, and `GATE_2_CALIBRATION.md` §1 and A11. It does not establish one intact ortholog per gene. The coding frame uses table 5 across all taxa, a locked choice with unmeasured sensitivity across other codes. The “negatives-only” fold procedure still chooses Codetta using the training positives and all 340 negatives. The tabulated 0/340 for its positive-lineage folds is guaranteed by its requirement that the chosen Codetta setting call no Y in those same negatives. Both settings have that property. This is distinct from the reported leave-one-phylum-out exercise, which still does not validate the full selection procedure on an untouched cohort. All **17** positive T1 rows have zero internal TAA and Codetta “?”. Thus “Stop” is an operational classification. The terminal TAA calls in the principal fish dossiers add corroboration, while weak, context-specific or very rare sense decoding remains outside the measured resolution.

`PREREG.md` §2.1, `src/gate3_post.py` lines 9–39 and 75–81, `src/gate3u_classify.py` lines 31–37, and `results/gate3_unverified/tests.txt` line 26. “Closest” takes accession-sorted records within a rank and excludes only the query accession, so repeated taxids can fill the quota. ## 6. Three Closing Questions

1. **What is the single weakest link?** The genome-level tRNA-Tyr locus adjudication. A single model-column stem judgment prevents the one strong Tyr candidate from counting, while a chromosome-local pass admitted a *Globodera* record with an alternative U33 locus elsewhere in the same mitogenome.
2. **Which headline is overstated?** 17/28,583 as a biological frequency or 17 validated counterexamples. It is a reproducible screen-positive rate. The narrow C33 finding in vertebrates survives. Code 5 and mtRF1a sufficiency remain unresolved.
3. **What single verification would most effectively stress-test the main conclusion?** Competitively map the existing *R. reniformis* WGS reads `SRR26324223` to `CM003310.1` and the nuclear assembly `GCA_001026735.1`, and locally assemble the whole trnY locus with mitochondrial flanks, blind to the reported stem result. Then compare the independently assembled locus with the Infernal and ARAGORN boundary maps under the registered criteria. A different locus, conflicting allele, or nuclear-only placement would overturn the proposed U33 genotype. A unique mitochondrial U33 trnY with supported boundaries would make the zero-new-clade result transparently dependent on the locked alignment implementation.
