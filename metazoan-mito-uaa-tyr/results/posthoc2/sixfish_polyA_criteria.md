# Six Tier 1 C33 fish: terminal stops and poly(A) ends, rules [P]

Post-hoc, written 2026-11-25 before any RNA read of this check was looked at. Nothing locked changes.

**Genome side (all six).** For each of the 13 protein genes the 3' end comes from a GenBank annotation: the counted record's own (`NC_037360`, `NC_013627`, `NC_013436`, `NC_013628`) or, for the two unannotated assembly records, annotated conspecific records lifted over by sequence (*A. glossodonta*: `AP002973`, `OZ556087`; *L. verrillii*: `QB104173`, `PX940805`, `PV339947`). Classes: complete UAA encoded (TAA inside the gene); incomplete, T-- or TA- (a tRNA or the next gene starts right after T or TA); UAG; AGA or AGG; other. ATP6 ending TA where the next gene is COX3 starting ATG is listed as TA- with a flag, because the genome reads TAA across the one-base overlap.
Independent check 1: the profile alignment of the sweep (`src/uaa_caller.py`) gives the frame and the last aligned codon; the first in-frame TAA, TAG, AGA or AGG after it, or a truncated T/TA at a tRNA or gene start. Independent check 2: the annotated protein is aligned (MAFFT) with U33 UAA=Stop RefSeq fish of the same or neighbouring orders; the end is "conserved" when it lies within 5 residues of the median reference end.

**RNA side.** A read supports a 3' end when it aligns to the gene's strand of the counted mitogenome with at least 30 matched bases and continues past the genomic match into at least 10 bases of which at least 90% are A (sense orientation), and that A run is not in the genome: at most 4 of the first 10 genomic bases after the junction are A. Because a genomic A next to the junction cannot be told from a tail A, junctions are placed at the leftmost position consistent with the read.
A gene's stop is **transcript-supported** when at least 5 reads (distinct read names) put the poly(A) junction at the annotated end (within 2 bases, or after the complete stop codon and before the next gene) and the transcript read in frame gives UAA (encoded, or T or TA plus A). Fewer reads, or a tail elsewhere, is reported as it is. A junction that gives UAG, AGA or AGG is reported as that codon. Species without RNA or TSA data: "annotation only". The RNA shows where the transcript ends and which codon stands there; it does not show which amino acid, if any, a ribosome would insert.

**Correction (2026-11-25, before any RNA read was looked at).** `AP002973` is dropped as an annotation source: it is filed as *A. glossodonta* but is 87% identical to the counted record (`results/gate3/notes_albula_records.md`), so it is not conspecific. The *A. glossodonta* ends are lifted over from `OZ556087` only (filed as *A. vulpes*, 99.8 to 100% identical to *A. glossodonta* in *CYTB*). A lift-over with less than 95% identity over the last 90 nt of the gene is not used.

**Extension (2026-11-25, after a first look).** The plan was the first 20,000,000 spots of each run. That look left several genes with fewer than 5 tail reads, so all spots of both runs were streamed. The rules did not change. A mate pair counts once. Counts from the first look are not used.
