# Prior art: C at position 33 of mitochondrial tRNA-Tyr in fish [P]

Searched 2026-11-25, before posting the preprint. Not part of any locked step.

**Found.** Satoh, Miya, Mabuchi & Nishida 2016, BMC Genomics 17:719, doi 10.1186/s12864-016-3054-y, Additional file 8 (Table S7, figshare 4475036, sha256 f2c9ddb5522477ee14c6a61278d60a26c8f3157ad98b0a44eb3cc37c09453e8f), sheet `Tyr`: in their numbering the anticodon is 40-42 and position 39 is canonical 33; at 39 the counts over 250 fishes are A 0, C 2, G 0, T 248. The species are not named there, and the main text does not discuss the site (keyword search of the full text). Their species list (Additional file 1) includes `AP002973` (filed as *Albula glossodonta*) and `AB051197` (*Pterothrissus gissu*, source of `NC_005796`); both are C33 in this study's calls, so they account for the two. Of the 249 species parsed from the list, 226 match ours by name: 215 U33, 2 C33 (these), 9 unresolved.
`AP002973` is not the counted *A. glossodonta* record (87% identical) and is never counted; *P. gissu* is Tier 3. Satoh's table therefore covers one of the four lineages (the bonefishes), not the eels, *Microdevario* or the eelpout.

**Not found.** No paper that names or discusses C33 in a fish tRNA-Tyr.
- Web searches (6 queries, 4 of them extended) on tRNA-Tyr position 33, C33 or U33C in fish, eels, bonefishes, *Microdevario* and eelpouts.
- Europe PMC: 13 targeted queries, then 511 open-access papers from 20 queries (fish, eel, elopomorph, zoarcid and danionin mitogenomes; mitochondrial tRNA position 33; queuosine; UAA reassignment); 492 had full text. 53 discuss position 33 of some tRNA; with tRNA-Tyr, only Li et al. 2019 (hemichordates, starfish).
- The *M. kubotai* (doi 10.3109/19401736.2014.936413, abstract only) and *Xyrias revulsus* (PMC7782292) mitogenome papers do not mention it.

**Limits.** Paywalled papers, drawn tRNA figures, supplementary tables other than Satoh's, theses and non-English papers were not searched. The sequences themselves have been in GenBank since 2000 or later.
