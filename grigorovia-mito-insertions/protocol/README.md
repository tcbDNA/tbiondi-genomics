# protocol/

The rules, the scan and its controls. Every number about the scan in `../README.md` and `../notebook.md` comes from
one of these files. `scripts/run_all.sh` regenerates them into `results/` (the scan's DIAMOND hit tables, about
800 MB, are left out and regenerated by `code/scan.py`).

## Rules and design
- `frozen_rules.md`: the pre-registered rules (section 0), the calibrated rules frozen before the scan (section 1), and the
  full timestamped log of every decision, correction and post-freeze addition. It ends with the commit hashes and
  times from the project's local git repository, which were **not externally timestamped**.
- `design.md`: the design agreed before any data were downloaded, and its four amendments.
- `prior_art.md`: prior work. Each source is labelled with how deeply it was read: A = abstract, K = keyword windows,
  F = full text.
- `accessions.tsv`: every record used: accession.version, set, taxid, organism, genetic code, length, and whether it
  was in the main scan.
- `corrections.md`: the fact-check and referee reviews of this release, every change they led to, and the table of file renames.

## scan/ (the frozen scan of 20,343 genomes)
- `genomes_refseq.tsv`, `genomes_fungal.tsv`: one row per genome: GC, hits, aligned bases, masked share, core genes
  hit, numbers of shift events and clean shifts, inferred inserts.
- `events_refseq.tsv`, `events_fungal.tsv`: every frameshift event. Columns: gene, reference protein, position,
  direction, placement-window codons (`codon_left`/`codon`, window width), class (clean or the filter that removed
  it), flank identities, whether it was within one alignment or between two hits.
- `inserts_*.tsv`: inferred introns and bypass-like inserts.
- `genome_flags.tsv`, `flags_summary.md`: the confirmatory single-genome flags, with E (expected chance results at
  least as extreme across the scan) and the strong mark.
- `pool_tests.tsv`, `shuffled_pools.tsv`: family and order pool tests, and the shuffled-label control.
- `flag_context.md`/`.tsv` (post-freeze, descriptive): per-flag sites, shift totals, platform and assembly, related
  genomes, independent assemblies, the flag's own chance, and the paper/record source of each site.
- `second_assembly_VdLs17.tsv`: the second-assembly step for *Verticillium dahliae* VdLs.17 (38 positions).
- `predictions.md`: the six predictions, scored by criteria fixed before the results.
- `completeness.md`: genomes expected, scored and not scored, per batch.
- `lineage_sensitivity.md`/`.tsv`: real-data sensitivity in the known lineages: documented sites, the outcome at
  each, per-lineage rates, *nad3*-174 separately, and out-of-sample sites.
- `many_shift_tally.md`/`.tsv`, `paired_break_tally.md`/`.tsv`, `removed_breaks_known_groups.md`,
  `tally_*.tsv`: exploratory tallies.
- `genome_coverage.tsv`, `coverage_by_phylum.tsv`: per-genome and per-phylum alignment coverage.
- `scan_report.md`: the report written by the stop rule right after the scan, with correction notes added at review.
- `scan_composition.tsv`: scanned genomes by group (animals, fungi, protists and algae) and source.

## controls/
- `BARS.md`, `thresholds.json`: the control re-run: headline sensitivity, scan gates, bars, stated limits, the
  misplacement and pool-site checks.
- `plants.tsv`, `null.json`: the planted null (24,660 planted shifts) and the codon vote rates derived from it.
- `sites_trials.json`: the 840 same-codon trials (2, 3 and 5 sites).
- `length_filter_precheck.log`: the reference-length filter test (not kept).
- `eval_window_convention.txt`: the offline evaluation, after the first run, that led to the window rule.

## audit/
- `known_junctions_refseq238.tsv`: every frameshift written into RefSeq mitochondrial CDS joins.
- `controls_known_cases.tsv`: the known cases used as controls.
- `annotation_summary.txt`: how records write frameshifts.

## search_log/
- `prior_art_search_log.md`: the queries behind `../prior_art.md`.
