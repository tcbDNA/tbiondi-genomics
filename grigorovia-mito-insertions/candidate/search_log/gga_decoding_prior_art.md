## What is documented (all A)

- **Potato mitochondria** encode only a tRNA-Gly(GCC), which the authors judge "likely to be insufficient to decode the four
  GGN glycine codons"; cytosolic tRNA-Gly(UCC) and tRNA-Gly(CCC) are found in the mitochondria (Brubacher-Kauffmann S,
  Maréchal-Drouard L, Cosset A, Dietrich A, Duchêne AM 1999, Nucleic Acids Res, doi:10.1093/nar/27.9.2037, PMID 10198438).
- **Tobacco mitochondria** import tRNA-Gly(UCC) and tRNA-Gly(CCC), while tRNA-Gly(GCC) stays in the cytosol (Salinas T,
  Schaeffer C, Maréchal-Drouard L, Duchêne AM 2005, Biochimie, doi:10.1016/j.biochi.2005.04.004, PMID 15927343).
- **Yeast mitochondria** (*S. cerevisiae*) import some nuclear-encoded tRNAs: tRNA-Lys(CUU) (Martin RP, Schneller JM,
  Stahl AJ, Dirheimer G 1979, Biochemistry, doi:10.1021/bi00588a021, PMID 387075) and tRNA-Gln (Rinehart J, Krett B,
  Rubio MA, Alfonzo JD, Söll D 2005, Genes Dev, doi:10.1101/gad.1269305, PMID 15706032).

## What is not claimed

These searches were not designed to show absence. The README therefore makes **no** claim that tRNA-Gly import, or
decoding of GGA by a GCC tRNA, is unknown in yeast or fungi. It cites the plant precedent and the yeast imports above,
and says that import in *G. jiainica* cannot be checked from DNA data.
