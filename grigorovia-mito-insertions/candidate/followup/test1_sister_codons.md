# Test 1: the congener's codons at the four sites (G. yakushimaensis OR703946; exploratory)

Tables: `test1_sister_codons.tsv` (one row per site) and `test1_placements.md` (every candidate placement, with
residues), both generated by `code/explore/grigorovia_h1h2.py sister`.

**Method.**
- For each site, every base within 3 nt of the extra base is removed in turn.
- The G. jiainica coding window (±30 nt) is aligned locally to OR703946, on both strands.
- Placements are ranked by score, then by mismatches. A site is comparable when the alignment is gapless and spans
  the site.

**At all four sites a placement exists in which the extra base sits right before GGC.**
- It is the A of AGG at cox1, cob 26496 and cob 26266, and a T after TGA at cob 26477.
- At cob 26266 that placement is not the best-scoring one, though it gives the same protein.

**Read against the logged prediction.**
- H1 predicted Gly in the congener at every comparable site.
- cob 26496 fits. At cob 26266 the congener has the same protein as every top placement.
- At cox1 the congener has Ser, which a G-skip reproduces. That is a point against the specific "skip the A" reading,
  not against a skip as such.
